This is the most beautiful bookshop I have ever visited in my life. It feels like stepping into a fairytale: the building is Bradford’s old Wool Exchange, all Venetian Gothic arches and stone and stained glass, and everything inside has been done meticulously to match.

## What’s there

A wide collection of books, as you’d hope, plus games, unusual stationery and gifts. The upstairs café is nothing short of stunning — tables set up on the gallery under the vaulted roof, looking down over the shop floor.

## What to do

Go, stick around, grab a coffee and cake in the café, catch up on some work or just sit and take in the surroundings. It’s half an hour from Leeds and makes a proper afternoon of it.

## The practical bit

- **Where:** Waterstones, The Wool Exchange, Hustlergate, Bradford BD1 1BL. A short walk from Bradford Forster Square or Interchange stations; around 30 minutes from Leeds by train.
- **Book?** No. Just turn up.
- **Go if:** you like books, buildings, or a very good excuse for cake.
