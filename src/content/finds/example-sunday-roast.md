There is a particular kind of Sunday where the rain is doing that fine sideways thing and the only reasonable response is to drive somewhere with a fire. This was one of those.

## What we had

The beef, pink and sliced thick, with a gravy that had clearly been started on Friday. Cauliflower cheese came as its own small dish and was fought over. The Yorkshire pudding needs no further comment beyond the one in the summary.

## What it's like

Low beams, mismatched chairs, a dog asleep under the next table. Nobody rushed us. The bill for two with a bottle of something red came to a little under £70, which for this felt fair.
